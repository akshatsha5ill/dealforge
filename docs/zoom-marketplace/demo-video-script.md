# Demo Video Script & Screenshot Guide

## Demo Video (60–90 seconds, MP4, 1080p)

**Concept**: "Meeting intelligence that keeps your data in your browser." Show a real meeting → analysis → follow-up, with a privacy beat.

| # | Shot | Duration | Narration |
|---|------|----------|-----------|
| 1 | **Hook** — Split screen: a sales rep in a Zoom meeting with DealForge panel open, vs. a wall of logos (Gong, Chorus, Otter) with "their servers" crossed out | 0:00–0:07 | "Every meeting intelligence tool stores your conversations on their servers. DealForge doesn't." |
| 2 | **In-meeting panel** — Live transcription appearing in the DealForge Zoom panel; switch to Suggestions tab | 0:07–0:20 | "DealForge runs inside Zoom — live transcription, and AI suggestions as the conversation happens." |
| 3 | **Dashboard** — After the meeting: the meeting appears automatically. Click Analyze → summary, action items, sentiment render. | 0:20–0:38 | "When the meeting ends, DealForge analyzes everything: summary, action items, sentiment." |
| 4 | **Lead scoring** — Show Leads page with BANT scores auto-populated from the conversation; highlight a hot lead. | 0:38–0:52 | "Prospects are scored automatically on BANT signals — so you know who to chase first." |
| 5 | **Follow-up email** — Emails page: AI-drafted follow-up referencing the meeting, one click to send. | 0:52–1:05 | "Draft the follow-up in one click, right from the meeting context." |
| 6 | **Privacy payoff** — DevTools IndexedDB view + "bring your own key" settings. End card: logo, URL, "Free tier available". | 1:05–1:20 | "And everything lives in your browser — or flows through your own AI key. Your data, your call. Get started free." |

**Production notes**:
- Use a scripted 3–4 minute fake sales call for realism; blur or fake names.
- Keep text overlays minimal and large; add captions.
- No music with lyrics; 1080p; under 90s if possible.
- End card includes the home URL.
